# Hello!
* I am a Research Assistant Professor and the Technical Director of the Institute for Intelligent Networked Systems (INSI), Northeastern University, Boston (<a href="/assets/pdf/polese-cv-2026-07.pdf" target="_blank">CV</a>). My research interests are on
	- next-generation AI-driven wireless networks, 5G/6G, AI-RAN, and Open RAN
	- open, intelligent, and programmable end-to-end network architectures
	- spectrum sharing and active/passive user coexistence
	- open-source networking software and experimental wireless testbeds
	- performance evaluation of end-to-end complex networks with system-level simulations.

	More on my <a href="/research/">research</a>: <a href="/research/dapps/">dApps and the E3 interface</a>, <a href="/research/ai-ran/">AI-RAN</a>, <a href="/research/spectrum-sharing/">spectrum sharing</a>, and <a href="/research/open6g/">Open6G</a>.

* I am the chair of the AI-RAN Alliance AI-and-RAN working group. My research on dApps and the E3 interface is now part of major commercial protocol stacks (NVIDIA 5G Aerial, Nokia, Cohere, Radisys). I am also co-founder of <a href="https://ztouchnet.com" target="_blank">zTouch Networks</a>, which won the Deutsche Telekom/T-Mobile T-Challenge 2026 and an AI-RAN Alliance Innovation Award, and lead <a href="https://open6g.us" target="_blank">Open6G</a> at Northeastern, the first O-RAN ALLIANCE recognized OTIC in the U.S. to have released a certificate for an O-RAN RU.

* Previously, I was Principal Research Scientist at Northeastern. I obtained my Ph.D. in 2020 at the University of Padova, Italy, with <a href="https://scholar.google.it/citations?hl=en&user=Z7d93ZYAAAAJ&view_op=list_works" target="_blank">Prof. Michele Zorzi</a>. During my Ph.D., I visited New York University (NYU), in April 2017, AT&T Labs in Bedminster, NJ, in May and June 2018, and Northeastern University in March-July 2019.

* Our <a href="https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/10024837" target="_blank">O-RAN tutorial (open access)</a> is one of most cited resources on this topic (1500+ citations). You can find more resources on the <a href="https://openrangym.com" target="_blank">OpenRAN Gym website</a>.
