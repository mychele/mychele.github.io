I lead [Open6G](https://open6g.us) at Northeastern University. Open6G is the first O-RAN ALLIANCE recognized Open Testing and Integration Center (OTIC) in the U.S. to have released a certificate for an O-RAN radio unit (RU). Open6G launched in 2022 with a grant from the U.S. Department of Defense's Innovate Beyond 5G program.

## Testbeds

* **X5G**: open, programmable, multi-vendor, end-to-end private 5G O-RAN testbed with NVIDIA ARC (Aerial) and OpenAirInterface. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.15935)
* **Northeastern private 5G network**: a [fully automated O-RAN private 5G network](https://www.rcrwireless.com/20230420/private-5g/northeastern-university-launches-o-ran-private-5g-network) launched in 2023.
* **[Colosseum](https://colosseum.net)**: the large-scale wireless network emulator, used as an Open RAN digital twin. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.17317)
* **[OpenRAN Gym](https://openrangym.com)**: open-source framework for AI/ML xApp and dApp design and testing on these platforms.
